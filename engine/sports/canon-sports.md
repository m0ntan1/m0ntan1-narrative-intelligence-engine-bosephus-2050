# Canon, sports

Settled beats of the sporting world on Bosephus's timeline. Separate from
`canon-2050.md` on purpose: SPORTS LOGIC will produce artifacts at a hundred
times the rate of ARTICLE mode, and letting ballgames into the world-history
Canon would turn every future artifact into a continuity-checking exercise. A
sports beat only graduates into `canon-2050.md` if it is genuinely
world-historic, which almost none of them are.

## Constructed, after the Present Line

**This section is deliberately empty and must be seeded by a human.**

The WVU conference arc across the 2026 to 2050 seasons is exactly the class of
thing the Locked Calendar doctrine exists to protect. A hallucinated realignment
would silently corrupt every college artifact written after it, and nobody would
catch it, because it would be internally consistent and completely made up.

So the first college run does not invent it. It proposes an arc in the artifact,
flags it as unseeded, and stops. Rick approves or corrects, the approved arc
lands here, and every run afterwards is bound by it.

Seed each entry as: season range, conference, and the one-line mechanism that
moved them. Mechanism is the point. "They joined the X" is worthless. "The 2029
media rights cycle repriced tier-two football and the conferences that survived
were the ones that owned their own distribution" is a claim you can argue with.
