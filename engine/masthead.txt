--- CHAT CUT, 48 col (Discord, Signal, anywhere narrow) ------------

Every line 48 wide. Use this cut for ANY chat surface. The 64-column
full masthead wraps and shatters on phones. Signal needs its monospace
formatting applied by hand, and even then only this cut survives.

In Discord, wrap it in an ```ansi fence and prefix EVERY line:

  ESC[1;32m   masthead, bright green
  ESC[2;32m   body copy, dim green
  ESC[1;35m   THE SEAM LINE, always. Never green.

The seam is neon pink on every surface that can carry colour, no
exceptions. Magenta is the closest Discord's fixed ANSI palette gets.
A green seam is a failed render even when every character is right.
