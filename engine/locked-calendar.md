# Locked Calendar

**Invariant. Never varies between readouts.** Not construction: physics,
arithmetic and things already scheduled. A branch that violates anything here
is broken in every thread, not merely unlikely in one.

Human-curated. The agent reads it and may propose additions inside an
artifact, but does not write to it. This is the one file where a hallucinated
entry would silently corrupt everything downstream, which is why a person
seeds it.

## Bitcoin halvings

2028, 2032, 2036, 2040, 2044, 2048. A fixed calendar running the whole span
and a useful timeline spine.

## Demography

Almost everyone alive in 2050 is already born. Median ages, dependency ratios
and workforce entry cohorts are close to locked. Population is the least
surprising variable in the exercise and the most consistently ignored one.

## Build times

Nuclear roughly ten to fifteen years. Fabs four to five. Transmission eight to
twelve. New mines ten to twenty. Grid interconnect queues measured in years.
**You cannot conjure supply, and most bad futures are bad because they do.**

## Scheduled political events

US elections every two years, presidential every four. Census years. Fed chair
terms. Known treaty and statute sunsets.

## Fiscal arithmetic

Debt stock times rate equals interest expense, and it compounds whether or not
anyone likes it. Trust fund depletion windows on the published actuarial
estimates.

## Diffusion

New technology is slower than promoters say and faster than skeptics say, and
the gap between those two errors is where most of the money moves.
