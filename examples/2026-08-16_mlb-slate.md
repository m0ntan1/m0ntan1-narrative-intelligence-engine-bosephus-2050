**Conditions worth naming.** Only two parks produced weather that
changes how the game gets played, and the rest are recorded and
ignored, which is the correct treatment of a mild August evening.

- **Wrigley Field.** West wind at 10, gusting 16, air carrying at 1.05
  against standard. West at Wrigley is the direction that turns a
  warning-track fly into a souvenir. That is why the Cubs number is
  seven and not four.
- **Oracle Park.** Sixty-three degrees, carry 1.014, the thinnest air
  advantage on the board and by some distance the least of it. Cool
  marine air off the bay is the reason a Colorado lineup that scores
  4.74 a game gets held to two.
- **Tropicana Field.** A dome. No conditions block exists for it and
  none should.

█▓▒░ HOW IT WENT ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**The five that went against the book.**

Detroit had the better bullpen and the better ballpark and lost anyway,
because Sean Burke had thrown 135 innings by the middle of August with
a 1.08 WHIP and that is not a fluke, that is a pitcher. Comerica gives
back nothing to a right-hander who keeps the ball down. Four to two,
and the Tigers never had more than one runner in scoring position after
the fourth.

Cincinnati is a bandbox and it was 86 degrees, which usually means the
home club scores. It also means the visiting club scores, and Miami had
Eury Perez while the Reds ran out a starter carrying a 1.46 WHIP.
Six to four in a game that felt closer than the line.

Sacramento was 86 and still, and the Athletics have allowed 5.79 runs a
game all year in a minor league park. There is no weather story here and
no narrative one either. Texas scored six because that is what happens.

Anaheim was the closest of the five. Kansas City has been bad and cold,
three of ten coming in, and the Angels had won six of ten. None of that
mattered next to a starter with a 6.71 ERA facing one at 4.45. Five to
four, and the two-run gap in ERA showed up as a one-run game, which is
the honest version of that edge.

Los Angeles was two aces and a coin. Henderson at a 0.91 WHIP against
Skubal at 0.93 is as close as two starters get. I took Milwaukee three
to two on the strength of a better record and nothing else, and I want
to be clear that this was the weakest call on the board.

**The five that went with it.**

New York took the Nationals five to three and I did not enjoy writing
it. Washington scores more than the Mets do, considerably more, and it
did not matter, because Jake Irvin has a 5.79 ERA and a hot club at
home does not need much. This is the whole discipline in one line: the
club I love had the better lineup and the worse pitcher, and the worse
pitcher decides more games than the better lineup does.

Minnesota and Philadelphia was two starters nobody wanted, 6.27 against
5.25, and it went six to five because games between bad pitching go
that way and the home side gets last licks.

Chicago beat St. Louis seven to five with the wind out. Cabrera was not
good. He did not have to be.

San Francisco held Colorado to two in the coldest air on the slate.
Blade Tidwell has only 22 innings on the year and I leaned on the park
more than the pitcher.

Houston beat Seattle five to three. The Mariners score 3.92 a game and
that is the lowest figure on the board. A club that cannot score does
not win in Houston in August with the roof shut.

█▓▒░ THE PIVOT ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Across a slate rather than a single game, the pivot is not an event, it
is a choice of method. **Every one of these ten calls was made on the
starting pitcher first and the lineup second.** Where those two
disagreed, the arm won, every time, including against my own club.

That is falsifiable in the cleanest way available. If this slate grades
badly, the method is what failed, not the individual reads, and the
correction is to weight offence higher. If it grades well, the rule
holds and the next slate is built the same way.

█▓▒░ THE FORKS ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**LOAD-BEARING.** Six or seven winners, margin inside two on about
half, and a small positive delta against the naive home pick. Starting
pitching in August is a real edge and a modest one.

**LIVE.** Five winners and no delta at all. Ten games is not enough to
separate method from noise, and a night where the favourites hold is
indistinguishable from a night where I was right.

**TAIL.** Three or fewer, which would mean the method is actively
worse than picking the home team, and the arm-over-lineup rule gets
rewritten rather than tuned. It needs the two ace matchups and the two
coin flips all to break the same way.
