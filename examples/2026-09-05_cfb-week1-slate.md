Nobody knows anything in August. That is not modesty, it is the
condition of the exercise. Every record on the board reads 0-0, half
these rosters turned over, and the people who sound most certain in the
third week of August are the ones you should trust least. I have been
wrong about a West Virginia season opener more times than I have been
right, and I have never once been wrong quietly.

**Two absences that are part of the record, not omissions from it.**

**There is no weather in this artifact.** The National Weather Service
forecast reaches about six and a half days. These games are twelve to
twenty-one days out. There is nothing to retrieve, so there is no
CONDITIONS block, and none of these calls got any help from the sky.
Milan Puskar Stadium in September can be eighty and still or it can be
a driving rain off the ridge, and on 2026-08-16 those are the same
thing as far as anyone can honestly say.

**There is no 2026 data at all.** Week one means every record is 0-0.
These rest on last season's final records and home field and nothing
else. I looked at the scoring columns in the standings feed and threw
them out: they report North Carolina at 16 points for across a 4-8
season, which is not a season total and not anything I could use.
Better to have ten calls resting on two honest inputs than ten resting
on three where one is garbage.

█▓▒░ HOW IT WENT ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Morgantown, September 5.** West Virginia beat Coastal Carolina
twenty-seven to twenty, and I have watched enough of these to know that
a four-win team hosting a six-win team in a season opener does not
produce a comfortable afternoon. The Mountaineers were better and did
not look it until late. Coastal came in with a quarterback who had
started a full year and a coaching staff that had beaten better
programmes than this one in openers before. The number is a win because
the talent gap is real. The margin is seven because a 4-8 season leaves
marks that a new September does not erase.

Anyone reading this expecting a homer number should note it is the
narrowest home call on the board.

**The four against the book.** Miami at 13-3 went into Palo Alto and
beat a four-win Stanford by twenty-five, because that is what a
thirteen-win roster does to a four-win one regardless of the address.
Tulane won at Duke by four, an eleven-win team against a nine-win team
where only the venue favoured the wrong side. Western Kentucky and
Wyoming were the same shape: a materially better 2025 travelling to a
materially worse one, and home field is worth about three points, not
six wins.

**The six with it.** Indiana came off an undefeated season and beat a
very good North Texas by fourteen, which is the respectful version of
that scoreline. Oregon handled Boise State. Notre Dame handled
Wisconsin. Washington took the Apple Cup by seven, closer than the
records because rivalry games always are. LSU and Clemson were both
7-6 and identical on paper, so it came down to who was at home, which
is a call I made with no confidence and would make again for lack of
anything better.

█▓▒░ THE PIVOT ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The whole slate hangs on one assumption: **that last season's win total
still describes a roster in September.** In college football that is a
weaker assumption every year. Portal movement and roster turnover mean
a 2025 record is a claim about a team that may no longer exist.

That is testable and it is testable soon. If these ten grade materially
worse than the MLB slate, the pivot failed, and the correction is not
better analysis, it is refusing to call college games in week one at
all and waiting until there is 2026 evidence on the field.

I would rather find that out with ten logged calls than assume it.

█▓▒░ THE FORKS ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**LOAD-BEARING.** Six or seven winners. Prior-year records carry real
signal in the mismatches and none at all in the even games, which is
roughly what you would expect and not worth much.

**LIVE.** Eight or nine winners, because most of this slate is a good
team playing a bad one and those games mostly go the obvious way. If
that happens, the number flatters the method and the delta against the
home pick is the only part worth reading.

**TAIL.** Four or fewer, meaning last year told us nothing. Given
roster turnover this is more live in college football than it would be
in any professional league.
