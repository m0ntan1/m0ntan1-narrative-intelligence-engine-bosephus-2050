proceed to the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act at 4:52 a.m. ET on
Saturday 2026-08-08, setting the first procedural vote for 2026-09-15
at 2:15 p.m. ET, seventeen days after Banking Committee Ranking Member
Elizabeth Warren called the rewritten text dead on arrival.

█▓▒░ I REMEMBER THIS ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

I read the cloture filing on a phone in a gravel lot outside
Morgantown, on a Saturday, because a filing at 4:52 in the morning
before a recess is a thing you do when you want the record to show you
tried. Everybody I knew read it as a whip count. Seven Democrats, find
them, done. That was the wrong frame and it took most of us the better
part of a year to put it down. The vote was never the constraint. The
constraint was that the one provision that would have bought those
seven votes could not be written while the man it described held the
office, and no amount of goodwill on the floor changes an arithmetic
problem that lives in the executive branch.

█▓▒░ WHAT HAPPENED NEXT ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

September 2026. Cloture failed. Not dramatically. It failed the way
most things fail in that chamber, which is that the managers counted
the night before, saw four and not eight, and let the vote happen
anyway so that everyone had something to run on. The ethics language
Tillis and Gallego had walked over to the White House never came back
in a form the seven could sign, because the only version that bought
Democratic votes required a sitting president to divest holdings he
had no intention of divesting, and no Republican manager was going to
put that on the floor and dare him. That is the whole mechanism. It is
not a story about crypto. It is a story about what happens when the
price of a bipartisan coalition is a personal cost to the executive.

Late 2026 into 2027. The vacuum did what vacuums do. The SEC and the
CFTC, both of which wanted the jurisdiction and neither of which
wanted to wait for Congress, moved on exemptive relief and joint
guidance, and the industry took it because a rule it could sue over
beat a statute it could not get. This was slower and uglier than
legislation and it produced the same practical outcome about four
years later, which is the base rate for regulatory questions Congress
declines to answer. Securities law has been settled by litigation
before. It took about a decade each time and it took a decade here.

2027 into 2028. The states filled in the rest, and the map went
patchwork, and the patchwork became its own lobbying argument for a
federal bill. Wyoming and a handful of others kept their charters.
New York kept its regime and kept extracting rent from it. The firms
that had spent 2025 and 2026 arguing that they needed federal clarity
discovered they could operate without it, at a cost, and once a cost
is survivable it stops being an emergency.

2029\. A market structure statute passed. It was narrower than CLARITY,
it was boring, and it passed for the reason these things always pass,
which is that the office the ethics objection had been aimed at
changed hands and the objection lost its object. The Democrats who had
blocked the 2026 text voted for a 2029 text that was, in the parts
that governed intermediaries, substantially similar. That is not
hypocrisy, it is the correct read of what they were actually objecting
to, and anyone who called it hypocrisy at the time was not listening.

2030 through the forties. Here is the part that disappointed
everybody. The statute mattered less than either side said it would.
It governed the intermediary layer, the exchanges and the custodians
and the brokers, and the intermediary layer consolidated the way
intermediary layers do, into a handful of institutions that looked
increasingly like banks and were eventually regulated like banks. The
protocol underneath was not a party to any of it. Bitcoin went through
the 2028 halving, the 2032 halving, the 2036, and the block schedule
did not consult the Federal Register once. People who had spent 2026
believing that US market structure law was an existential question for
the asset were making a category error, and it was an expensive one,
because it kept them watching the wrong screen for four years.

What the fight actually settled, and this took me twenty years to see,
was not the regulatory perimeter. It was that self-dealing by the
executive became the permanent frame for financial legislation. Every
market bill after 2026 carried an ethics title, because the 2026 fight
proved that the ethics title was the price. That is Warren's mark on
the record. She lost the bill and set the template, and the template
outlived every specific provision anyone argued about that summer.

█▓▒░ THE PIVOT ░▒▓█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Whether presidential divestment language appeared in the text brought
to the floor before the 2026-09-15 cloture vote. Not whether ethics
language existed in some draft, which it did, but whether it reached
the operative text with divestment in it. If it had, the seven were
gettable and the bill moved in the 119th. It did not, so they were
not, and it did not.

What it would have taken to go the other way: the White House
accepting a divestment requirement, or eight Democrats deciding that a
statute without one was better than agency rulemaking. Both were
possible on 2026-08-16 and both required somebody to absorb a loss in
public. That is a watchable event. Anyone reading the manager's
amendment in the first two weeks of September 2026 knew the answer
before the vote was called.

LOAD-BEARING. The branch above. Cloture fails, no statute in the
119th, agencies and states fill the gap, a narrower bill lands around
2029 once the ethics objection loses its object.

LIVE. Divestment language lands in the manager's amendment in early
September. Eight Democrats cross. CLARITY clears the Senate in the
fall of 2026, goes to conference over the Agriculture title, and is
signed in the first quarter of 2027. Materially better for the
intermediaries and roughly neutral for everyone else, since the
substantive text was never the fight.

TAIL. A large intermediary fails before the statute exists, in the
2027 or 2028 window, with customer assets involved. Congress then
writes the bill in ninety days and writes it angry, and the result is
materially worse than CLARITY for every firm that spent 2026 lobbying
against CLARITY. Needs a specific failure at a specific size. If a top
five US custodian discloses a material shortfall, move this to live.

Stop counting votes and go read the manager's amendment. The whole
question is whether the word divestment is in the operative text
before September 15, and if it is not there by the second week of
September then the September 15 vote is theater and the real
regulatory perimeter gets drawn by two agencies and a circuit court
over the following four years. Also, and I say this as gently as I can
from where I am sitting: the protocol was never on the ballot. Bitcoin
closed down 4.6 percent across the loudest three weeks of this fight,
which is the market telling you plainly that it had already priced the
answer and the answer was that this mattered to intermediaries and not
to the thing itself. Educate. Disintermediate. Innovate. Build.
